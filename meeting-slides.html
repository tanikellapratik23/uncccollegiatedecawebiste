Main points

What we covered together.

A quick recap for anyone who joined us - and anyone who wants to get involved next.

01

Welcome to the club

Collegiate DECA turns business interests into competition experience, career-ready skills and meaningful connections with other students and professionals.

02

Competition builds confidence

Members can compete individually or with a team in marketing, management, finance, hospitality and entrepreneurship events.

03

Our chapter keeps growing

Fourteen members competed at ICDC in 2025 with six finalists. In 2026, twenty members competed and eight reached the final round.

04

We need fundraising volunteers

We are looking for members to help plan and staff fall fundraising, including the Autumn Bake Sale and future live activities.

Volunteer for fundraising →

Looking ahead

Learn, prepare, fundraise - then take on Austin.

The chapter is working toward ICDC 2027 at the JW Marriott Austin, April 9-12. Fall meetings will include competition preparation, interview practice, team building, fundraising and networking.
